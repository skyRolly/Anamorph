## N2-01/V2-01: owner decision

[The detailed decision package](H4_DRY_RECONSTRUCTION_0.10.2.md) contains all
candidate families, references, measured DSP/costs, recovery controls, rejected
alternatives, architecture implications and portable replay instructions.
At 20 Hz/48k/128, three seconds full wet then Mix .5 reproduces peak .136108398438,
RMS .101481723183 and -2.56591928782 dB over 960 samples; recovery above 16 float
eps lasts through sample 6513. The 50 Hz counterpart gives peak .054557144642 and
-.470427233122 dB. These are transient reconstruction errors, not stationary
response, listening evidence or native DAW incidence.

Recommend **A2: maintain the existing dry filters and aligned history, preserve
H4's exact-wet shortcut/clean Measure reference, and guard otherwise unused dry
state with channel-local recovery**. It changes no approved graph or latency.
The maintainer must accept its extra steady work and scoped recovery policy.
Measured whole-engine instructions rise **15.3/27.4/36.9%** at 48k/128 for 2/3/4
bands; sustained-glide comparisons also quantify the actual per-sample branch.
Shared-machine wall/thread-CPU variability prevents a universal native CPU or
instance-count claim. There is no replay spike or new buffer allocation.

Unguarded A/C ordinary diagnostics are insufficient: finite extreme input leaves
eight invalid unused dry states through 1.28 s of valid wet audio, then loses a
512-frame block on Mix re-entry. A2 executes four local heals per failed channel,
zero final-wet heals and no whole-block dip loss. Its valid right-channel state
matches 4/4; resetting both channels changes only that preservation condition to
0/4. These targeted results do not establish every extreme/latency interaction.

Filters-only warming fails aligned history at nonzero latency. Finite short
replay does not establish arbitrary IIR state equivalence and can create expensive
re-entry work; reduced-output C saves <.6% while adding interface complexity.
Copying wet state/changing filter representation lacks equivalence. Masking cold
output or delaying Mix changes the contract. Retain-with-explicit-limitation or
deferral remain owner options. ADR-0074 is **Proposed**, never agent-accepted.

V2-01's old "rings stay warm; re-engage is comb-free" claim exceeded Test 25's
noise/default-crossover aggregate metric. Live text now states that boundary;
its **trRatio>.90 and settled ratio>.95 assertions remain intact**. An optional
8-assertion low-frequency oracle checks real H4 gating, re-entry, first-window
error and recovery: original 8/2, A2 8/0, never-gate 8/2 failing only execution
premises. A mandatory failing test is not merged ahead of a production correction.
