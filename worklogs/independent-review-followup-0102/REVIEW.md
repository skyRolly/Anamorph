## H4 / N2-01 / V2-01 / ADR-0074

N2-01 and V2-01 remain P2; no production H4 change is authorized. Existing evidence attributes the low-frequency re-entry error to skipped recursive dry state, separate from ring history and coefficient synchronization. Test 25's unchanged ten-block aggregate noise RMS checks do not prove worst-frequency/sample-level reconstruction. The optional low-frequency oracle compares a continuously maintained independent dry module and full-wet reference, asserts gate and consumption premises, fails baseline and discriminates a never-gate control; it remains nonshipping until an approved fix. Its coverage is finite, and historical probe provenance is bounded as above.

New isolated and actual-engine witnesses show A2 can leave a non-finite dry recursive state with a finite unused dry output at a gated block boundary. In the 48 kHz/128 two-band extreme-input case the monitored dry/wet outputs were finite, one left dry state invalid and the right dry states finite. The next left block was all zero; a dry-channel reset and a no-Mix-dip control both still produced that zero block, so its cause is **not isolated to dry re-entry**. A nonshipping state-scan variant detects the hidden state in this one case but its broader recovery, unrelated-state behavior and cost are unmeasured. `worklogs/h4-review-followup-0102/` on #240 contains source, patches, command/binary identities, controls, limits and exact outputs. A2's output-only recovery guard is therefore insufficient as a general state-finiteness guarantee; no candidate is proven complete.

Historical whole-engine instruction ratios for A2 at 48 kHz/128 are +15.3%, +27.4%, +36.9% for 2/3/4 bands; actual x4 wrapping and sustained glide have separately recorded rows. Instructions, thread CPU, wall distributions, deadlines and instance capacity are not interchangeable. C's measured output-discard saving was below .6% whole-engine instructions; finite short-history reconstruction failed selected low-cutoff/high-rate cases; restricting the gate to one band reduces affected multiband cases to warming. These facts do not exclude a different mathematically proven implementation. This virtualized shared Linux host (Xeon Platinum 8573C, 3 vCPUs, GCC 14.2) is not representative native hardware and supplies no real-time deadline or DAW capacity evidence. No new representative timing or complete performance matrix was measured here; the portable benchmark procedure and historic raw data remain in the H4 packet.

Owner choices remain: accept A2 with its measured overhead and explicitly unproven recovery boundary; accept a different candidate only after supporting correctness/recovery/cost evidence; continue targeted investigation; retain H4 with the documented limitation; or defer. Recommendation: continue investigation, especially state inspection and valid-wet controls at block boundaries, inactive/active/fading/gliding banks, band-count changes, wrapped latency/ring history, repeated recovery and re-entry. Measure a proven candidate on representative hardware. ADR-0074 remains Proposed; merging documentation would not accept it.
